FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is the free domain really free?
Yes. One custom domain, whether a .com, .in, .shop, .co or .online, is included free with every paid Neweb plan for as long as your subscription stays active. We pay the registrar fee for the first year, and renewals after that are charged at cost, which is around 999 rupees for a .com and 599 rupees for a .in at current registrar rates. There is no markup and no renewal-time price hike. Crucially, the domain is registered in your own legal name with WHOIS privacy enabled, not under an agency account, so you always control it. You can transfer it out to any other registrar any time after the 60-day ICANN lock period that applies to every newly registered domain. The domain is yours, not ours.
What exactly does “online presence manager” mean?
An online presence manager goes beyond building a website. Neweb registers your domain, builds and hosts your website, claims and verifies your Google Business Profile, sets up SEO, and connects your newsletter, then keeps all of those surfaces in sync from one dashboard. The practical benefit is that you change a fact once and it updates everywhere. Edit your hours, your phone number, a photo, or a service, and Neweb pushes the change to your website, your Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Maps, and your newsletter together. Most small businesses today juggle a website builder, a separate domain registrar, a manual Google Business listing, and a disconnected email tool, which is where inconsistencies creep in. Neweb unifies the whole stack so every surface a customer sees tells the same story, which is what builds trust and local ranking over time.
Is it really WordPress — or just something that looks like it?
It is real, vanilla WordPress running on PHP 8.3 with a MySQL database. You get a genuine wp-admin login, your own database, your own theme, and access to the 60,000-plus plugins in the official WordPress directory. Neweb is the AI composer that sits on top and does the building, editing, and tuning for you, but the engine underneath is the same WordPress that powers roughly 43 percent of the web. This matters for two reasons. First, you are never locked into a proprietary builder that traps your content, because you can export to any WordPress host with one click and take your data, your domain, and your theme with you. Second, you inherit the entire WordPress ecosystem of plugins and developers if you ever outgrow the AI workflow. You get the openness of WordPress with the simplicity of a managed product.
Do I need to know anything about WordPress?
Nothing at all. The vast majority of Neweb customers never open wp-admin, and they do not need to. You build your site by answering a short four-question onboarding form, and you make every change after that by filling small forms or by typing what you want in plain English, such as add a gallery with these six photos or make the contact section shorter. The AI handles the WordPress mechanics underneath, including the theme, the blocks, the SEO markup, and the performance tuning. If you do happen to know WordPress, the full wp-admin is there for you to use, with your own database and plugin access. But if you have never touched it, you can run your entire website, publish blog posts, add pages, and change your layout without ever learning what a WordPress dashboard looks like. That is the point of the product.
What about SEO and performance?
Every Neweb site ships with the technical SEO foundations done for you: a 90-plus LiteSpeed performance score, valid schema.org structured data, auto-generated meta titles and descriptions, automatically created Open Graph social images, a clean XML sitemap, a tuned robots.txt, and alt text on every image. Hosting runs on LiteSpeed with edge caching and HTTP/3, so pages typically load in under one second even on a 3G connection, which matters because most Indian visitors are on mobile. On the Growth plan, SEO Autopilot goes further: it watches your Google Search Console data and rewrites your on-page content weekly to chase the queries you are close to ranking for. The result is that the slow, technical SEO work that usually needs an agency retainer is handled in the background, and your Core Web Vitals stay green without you touching a line of code.
Can I use my own domain?
Yes, on every paid plan. If you already own a domain from another registrar such as GoDaddy, Namecheap, or BigRock, you simply point it at Neweb by updating the nameservers or the A record, and we provision the SSL certificate and the CDN automatically within a few minutes. There is no extra charge for connecting an existing domain, and you keep ownership of it at your current registrar. If you do not have a domain yet, you can take the free one included with your plan instead, registered in your own name. You can also do both: keep your existing domain as the primary address and register the free Neweb domain as a backup or a redirect. Either way, your SSL is always provisioned and auto-renewed, so your site stays secure with an A-grade certificate without any manual work on your side.
Do you own my content or data?
No. Your content, your images, and your customer data belong entirely to you, not to Neweb. We never train AI models on your content, and we never sell or share your customer data. You can export your full website to any WordPress host in one click at any time, and you can transfer your domain out after the standard 60-day lock, so there is no lock-in holding your business hostage. On compliance, Neweb is built to SOC 2 Type II practices, is GDPR aligned for any European visitors, and is compliant with India Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 for your Indian customers. You can choose data residency in India, the United States, or the European Union depending on where your customers are. In short, we host and manage your presence, but you own every part of it.
