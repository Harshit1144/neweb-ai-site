Neweb / About

We build presence, not just websites.

Neweb is the small-business online presence manager — a website, a domain, a Google listing, and the discipline to keep them all in sync. Built in Ahmedabad for the next hundred million entrepreneurs.

Our story

We started Neweb in 2024 because a friend — a third-generation jeweller in Jaipur — spent ₹84,000 on a website that went offline in four months and didn't rank for his own name. He didn't need a website. He needed a presence.

Every small business in India and beyond runs a quiet, distributed marketing operation — a site, a Google listing, a phone number on a signboard, a half-claimed Bing Places. When one of them drifts out of sync, customers stop showing up. When you run a bakery, you shouldn't also have to run a CMS, a hosting provider, a domain registrar, an SEO consultant and a content team.

So we built one product that does all of it. Fill a four-question form. Neweb registers your free domain, generates a fast WordPress site, claims your Google Business Profile, tunes your SEO every week, and keeps every surface consistent. Forever.

We're a small team of engineers, designers, and operators who've spent years building for Indian small businesses. We believe the web should be owned, not rented. Your domain, your content, your data, exportable any time.

Today, Neweb powers 1,000+ small businesses across India — jewellers, bakeries, tutors, clinics, boutique hotels, wedding planners. We're bootstrapped, profitable, and hiring across engineering, design, and GTM — see careers.

What we believe

Four stubborn principles.

01

You own your presence.

Your domain is registered in your name. Your content exports to WordPress in one click. No lock-in, no platform risk, no hostage ransom.

02

Performance is table stakes.

Every site hits 90+ on LiteSpeed. Sub-1-second loads on 3G, even in Tier-2 cities. If we can't hit it, we don't ship it.

03

Simple beats configurable.

Four questions. One subscription. No tier upsells, no hidden add-ons. If a feature needs a tutorial, it's not a feature.

04

Small businesses first.

Every decision optimizes for a 3-person bakery, not a 300-person brand. If the owner doesn't understand it, we rewrote it.
