Questions from Mumbai businesses.

  • Can I get a Mumbai business website up in a single day?

    Yes, you can. Neweb generates your site in under a minute, registers a free domain, and sets up your Google Business Profile automatically, so most Mumbai owners finish the whole thing during a single local train commute. You describe your business, a Bandra cafe, an Andheri dental clinic, a Dadar chemist, or a Lower Parel boutique, and the site comes back ready with your services, photos, and contact details. You then edit the text, add your UPI QR code, link your WhatsApp Business number, and publish the same day. There is no developer to brief and no waiting weeks for revisions, which matters in a market as fast-moving as Mumbai where a Linking Road shop or a Powai salon cannot afford to stay invisible. Plans start at ₹249 per month with the domain and Google Business setup included. Tip: keep your shop photos and price list handy before you start so you can finish in one sitting.

  • Does Neweb support Marathi for my Mumbai website?

    Yes. Neweb publishes in Marathi and Hindi at no extra cost, alongside English. Each language version gets its own SEO indexing, so a Dadar sweet shop can reach Marathi-speaking locals while a Navi Mumbai gym still ranks for Hindi and English searches. This matters in Mumbai because customer language shifts street by street: Marathi dominates in Girgaon, Parel, and Borivali, while Hindi and English carry more weight in Bandra, Andheri, and the corporate belt of Lower Parel. A bilingual site lets a Ghatkopar kirana store or a Powai Hiranandani salon speak to each customer in the language they actually search and shop in. You can switch a page between languages with one click and keep prices in rupees throughout. Tip: write your most important pages, your services and your contact page, in Marathi first if your core customers are neighbourhood locals, then add English for wider reach.
