01
Neweb vs BigRock · 01

BigRock is India's trusted domain registrar. Its website builder is an afterthought.

BigRock has been a trusted name in Indian domain registration and web hosting since 2010. Millions of Indian businesses have registered their .in and .com domains through BigRock, and for this use case — buying and managing domains, setting up cPanel hosting — BigRock does the job reliably at competitive prices. It is a legitimate part of the Indian hosting ecosystem.

The website builder, however, was added to the product line to compete with global players rather than to serve a deep understanding of what Indian small businesses need from an online presence. The templates are limited and dated. The editing experience is basic. There is no SEO automation, no Google Business integration, no multilingual publishing, and no UPI or WhatsApp support. BigRock's website builder does enough to put a page on the internet; Neweb does enough to build a business presence that generates customers.

03
Neweb vs BigRock · 03

What BigRock does that Neweb does not

BigRock's domain management and hosting infrastructure are its strongest points. If you manage many domains, BigRock's registrar interface is mature and familiar to many Indian IT professionals. BigRock also offers cPanel hosting with root-level access, which allows advanced users to install and configure custom software — something Neweb's managed platform does not expose.

BigRock's email hosting options (particularly the business email powered by Google Workspace) are well-integrated with their domain management. For businesses that already have BigRock domains and email and prefer to keep everything with one Indian provider for billing simplicity, BigRock has a convenience argument. However, keeping your domain at BigRock and your website on Neweb is a valid and common setup — the two are not mutually exclusive.

What Neweb does that BigRock does not

Neweb automates Google Business Profile setup and keeps it synced with your website. BigRock's website builder has no GBP integration. Neweb runs weekly SEO tunes. BigRock has no automated SEO. Neweb publishes in 14 Indian languages. BigRock supports no multilingual publishing. Neweb ships with UPI QR codes, WhatsApp enquiry buttons, and GST invoice generation. BigRock has none of these natively.

Neweb's templates are modern, fast, and tuned for Indian business categories. BigRock's templates are dated and generic. Neweb delivers Core Web Vitals of 90+ on every site using LiteSpeed hosting and edge caching. BigRock's website builder does not publish performance benchmarks for its sites. Neweb support runs in IST hours with fast response. BigRock's website builder support is pooled with their general hosting support queue.

05
Neweb vs BigRock · 05

Keep your BigRock domain and switch to Neweb

You do not need to transfer your domain away from BigRock to use Neweb. The most common migration path is: keep your domain registered at BigRock, update the DNS nameservers in BigRock's domain management to point at Neweb's servers, and rebuild your website on Neweb. DNS propagation takes 24 to 48 hours. After that, your BigRock domain serves your Neweb website, and you continue managing domain renewal through your existing BigRock account.

If you prefer to transfer the domain to Neweb's registrar, we support that too. Transfers take 5 to 7 days and incur no additional cost on our side. Neweb will include the domain's annual renewal in your subscription from the point of transfer. The net effect is that your domain becomes cheaper to maintain — it's included rather than a separate annual line item.

Neweb vs BigRock · 06

The template quality gap

Website templates signal trust to customers. A modern, fast-loading template that presents your business professionally is not a cosmetic detail — it directly affects whether a visitor calls or clicks away. BigRock's website builder templates were designed several years ago and have not received significant updates. They are functional but lack the design language that customers in 2026 associate with professional businesses.

Neweb's templates are designed for specific Indian business categories: jewellers, clinics, restaurants, coaching centres, beauty salons, and more. Each template includes the page sections that matter for that category — a restaurant template has a menu section, a booking enquiry, and a location map. A clinic template has doctor profiles, a consultation booking flow, and a conditions treated section. The industry-specific design reduces setup time and produces a site that resonates with customers in your category from day one.
