Neweb at ₹249 per month is priced for India. The domain is free every year, not just the first year. When you compare what ₹249 delivers on Neweb versus what ₹750 delivers on Jimdo, the value gap is clear: Neweb includes features that Jimdo's highest-tier plan does not have at all.

Neweb vs Jimdo · 03

What Jimdo does that Neweb does not

Jimdo's biggest differentiator is its AI-generated first draft. The Dolphin builder reduces the initial website creation time to under 10 minutes for a typical small business. For a business owner who has been putting off getting online because "it's too complicated", Jimdo's AI onboarding removes that barrier. The resulting site is not particularly powerful, but it exists and it's live — which is better than nothing.

Jimdo also has a clean, minimalist editing interface that some users find easier than more feature-rich builders. The focus on simplicity means the learning curve is low. For businesses that want the simplest possible site with minimal ongoing interaction with the dashboard, Jimdo's stripped-back approach has a real user experience appeal.

What Neweb does that Jimdo does not

Neweb automates Google Business setup and syncing. Jimdo has no GBP integration. Neweb runs weekly SEO tunes. Jimdo's SEO is limited to basic meta tag editing. 05
Neweb vs Jimdo · 05

Local SEO: the Jimdo gap

For an Indian small business, local SEO — appearing in "near me" searches on Google Maps and in local organic results — often drives more customers than any other channel. Local SEO depends on three things: a well-structured website with correct schema, an active and verified Google Business Profile, and consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) data across the web.

Jimdo handles the first item adequately. It handles the second and third item not at all. There is no GBP integration, no citation management, and no automated NAP consistency checking. Neweb handles all three: schema is automatically generated from your business data, GBP is claimed and synced automatically, and NAP consistency is maintained as part of the weekly SEO tune. For an Indian business that needs to appear in local searches, Neweb's local SEO infrastructure delivers results that Jimdo cannot replicate without significant manual effort.

Neweb vs Jimdo · 06

Indian context: Jimdo's missing localisations

Jimdo is a German company that has localised its product for European and North American markets. India is an afterthought in the product design. There are no Hindi or regional language templates, no Indian payment rails, no WhatsApp integration, no GST tools, and no support for the local directories (JustDial, Sulekha, IndiaMart) that Indian business customers check. The AI-generated site will use generic global business language rather than the tone and content that resonates with Indian customers.

Neweb's default templates, content suggestions, and onboarding questions are all calibrated for Indian business types and Indian customer behaviour. A jeweller in Surat, a coaching institute in Kota, and a clinic in Hyderabad each get templates and defaults that match how their customers search, what their customers expect on a local business website, and the payment and communication methods their customers prefer. That localisation is invisible in a feature list but visible in the outcomes.
