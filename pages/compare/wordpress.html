Neweb vs WordPress · 01

WordPress is software. Neweb is a managed service.

WordPress.org is open-source software that you install on a web server. It has no inherent hosting, no bundled domain, no built-in SEO automation, and no support team. Everything comes from plugins and hosting providers you choose and pay for separately. This freedom is the point — WordPress can be customised into almost anything, and a skilled developer can build a website on WordPress that competes with any platform on earth.

The friction is the maintenance. A typical WordPress site for an Indian SMB involves a hosting account, a domain registrar, a page builder plugin, an SEO plugin, a security plugin, a caching plugin, a backup plugin, and often a form plugin and an email marketing plugin. Each needs to be updated regularly. Plugin conflicts happen. Hosting bills vary. Security breaches hit unmaintained WordPress sites routinely. Neweb handles all of this as a managed service — you get the outcomes without the overhead.

The real cost of WordPress

WordPress software is free. Running it is not. A basic setup requires: shared hosting at ₹100 to ₹400 per month, a domain at ₹800 to ₹1,200 per year, a premium theme at ₹3,000 to ₹8,000 (one-time or annual), an SEO plugin like Yoast Premium at ₹7,500 per year, a multilingual plugin like WPML at ₹3,500 per year, a security plugin at ₹2,000 per year, and backup storage. Before you've built a single page, the annual cost exceeds ₹25,000 for a properly set-up WordPress site.

WordPress.com (the hosted version) is cheaper to start — plans begin at about ₹300 per month — but features are restricted until you reach the Business plan at approximately ₹1,200 per month, which allows plugins. Even then, hosting quality, performance, and the managed workflow are inferior to Neweb. Neweb at ₹249 per month includes everything needed for a complete, fast, SEO-optimised Indian business website with no plugin shopping required.

Neweb vs WordPress · 03

What WordPress does that Neweb does not

WordPress's flexibility ceiling is essentially infinite. A skilled developer can build anything on WordPress — complex membership sites, multi-vendor marketplaces, advanced booking systems, custom CRMs, and niche integrations that no SaaS platform will ever support. The plugin ecosystem has over 60,000 free and premium plugins. If you need something specific, someone has probably built a WordPress plugin for it.

WordPress also gives you complete data ownership and portability. Your content lives in a database you control on a server you rent. You can move it anywhere, at any time, with no vendor lock-in. For businesses with a developer on staff or budget to hire one, WordPress's customisation depth is a genuine advantage. For businesses without technical resources, that same flexibility becomes a maintenance burden and a security liability.

What Neweb does that WordPress does not

Neweb automates Google Business Profile setup and weekly syncing — something WordPress cannot do without custom development or a paid service. Neweb runs weekly SEO tunes automatically; WordPress requires a plugin and someone to act on its recommendations. Neweb publishes in 14 Indian languages with correct hreflang markup out of the box; WordPress needs WPML or Polylang, which add cost and complexity.

Neweb ships with UPI QR codes, WhatsApp enquiry buttons, and GST invoice generation built-in. WordPress needs custom plugins or development for each of these. Neweb's LiteSpeed hosting and edge CDN deliver Core Web Vitals of 90+ on every site without any optimisation work; WordPress sites routinely score in the 50s to 70s without careful optimisation. And Neweb's IST-hours support team is available in minutes, not the hours or days that come with hosting provider support or WordPress forums.

Migrating from WordPress to Neweb

Migrating from WordPress to Neweb is the most common migration we handle. We export your WordPress posts and pages as XML, import the content into Neweb, rebuild the page structure using Neweb templates, and redirect your domain's DNS to Neweb servers. For a typical 10 to 20-page small business site with a blog, this takes 4 to 8 hours. On the Growth plan, our team handles the migration; on Starter, we provide a step-by-step guide.

The most common motivations for migrating away from WordPress are: ongoing maintenance burden, a security incident, a performance audit showing poor Core Web Vitals, or a recognition that plugin costs have exceeded the value delivered. After migration, the monthly cost typically drops and the site's Lighthouse score improves within the first week as LiteSpeed caching and our CDN take over from whatever the WordPress hosting was using.

Neweb vs WordPress · 06

Security: the silent WordPress problem

WordPress powers 43% of the web, which makes it the most targeted CMS by hackers. Outdated plugins, weak passwords, or unpatched themes are the most common attack vectors. Indian WordPress sites are routinely compromised and used for spam, phishing, or crypto mining — often without the business owner knowing for weeks. Cleaning up a hacked WordPress site costs between ₹5,000 and ₹25,000 with a developer, and the reputational damage from being blacklisted by Google can take months to reverse.

Neweb is a managed platform. Security is handled at the infrastructure level — automatic updates, WAF rules, brute force protection, and malware scanning are all included. There is no exposed wp-admin endpoint, no plugin vulnerabilities, and no user-managed update schedule. For an Indian small business owner who does not want to think about security, Neweb's managed model is meaningfully safer than self-hosted WordPress.
