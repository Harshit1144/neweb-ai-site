How fast do you actually reply?

Support averages 18 minutes, 24/7. Sales in under 2 hours. Press and partnerships within one business day. If you haven't heard back in 24 hours, something broke — email hello@neweb.ai and it'll get rescued.

Which payment methods do you accept?

UPI, all major Indian credit and debit cards, net banking, and NEFT for annual plans. All billing in ₹INR with GST invoicing. International customers can pay via card — we'll bill in INR and your card will handle the conversion.

Do you offer phone support?

For plans at ₹2,999/mo and above, yes — with a named account manager and a direct line. Otherwise, our chat and email are faster than any IVR.

I'm a journalist on deadline.

Email press@neweb.ai with "URGENT" in the subject line and your deadline in the body. We'll reply within 2 hours, weekends included.
