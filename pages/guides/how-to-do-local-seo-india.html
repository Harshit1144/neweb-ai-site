How to do local SEO for your small business in India.

⏱ 6 min read 📅 Updated June 2026 ✍ By Neweb team

Local SEO means making your business easy to find when people nearby search for what you offer. In India, "near me" searches have grown fast (Google, 2023), and voice search in regional languages is now common. So local SEO is no longer optional for any business with a physical location. This guide covers the 6 steps that move the needle.

01

Audit and fix your NAP consistency

NAP stands for Name, Address, Phone. It is the most basic local SEO signal. Google checks your business details across dozens of sources. This is how it confirms you are a real, stable business at a real location. Say your name shows up as "Sharma Electronics" in some places and "Sharma Electronics & Home Appliances" in others. Or your phone number is off by one digit between your website and JustDial. When that happens, Google trusts your listing less and your ranking drops.

Start by searching for your business name in Google, JustDial, IndiaMart, Sulekha, and Bing. Note every variation in your business name, address format, and phone number. Then standardise. Pick one official version and update every listing to match exactly. Use the same abbreviations (or spell them out), the same floor/flat format, and the same phone number format (+91 XXXXX XXXXX throughout).

  • Create a NAP reference document: the single source of truth you copy-paste into every directory
  • Include your GSTIN in your NAP document. Some Indian directories ask for it, and it adds legitimacy.
  • Check your website footer, contact page, and schema markup all match your NAP document exactly
  • Update WhatsApp Business, Facebook Business page, Instagram bio, and LinkedIn company page too
  • Neweb automatically generates LocalBusiness schema markup on your website using your verified NAP
02

Optimise your Google Business Profile fully

Google Business Profile is the highest-ROI local SEO tool for Indian small businesses, and it is free. The Local Pack is the box of three business listings at the top of Google results for local queries. It captures a large share of all clicks on the page (BrightLocal, 2023). If your business is not in the Local Pack for your main keyword, you are invisible to many of your potential customers.

Cover the basics first: NAP, hours, photos, and verification. Then go further. Choose the most specific primary category you can. Add secondary categories. Write a keyword-rich business description and use terms like "best X in [city]" naturally. Add every service you offer in the Services section. Post at least twice a month. Ask for reviews and reply to them. If you have not set this up yet, start with our guide on how to get a Google Business listing. Then use how to rank on Google Maps in India to climb the Local Pack.

  • Complete every attribute Google offers for your category: wheelchair access, free Wi-Fi, accepts UPI, women-led, and so on
  • Use the GBP Q&A section to pre-answer the 5 questions your customers ask most
  • Post Google Posts with seasonal offers, new products, or events. Active listings rank higher than dormant ones.
  • Add all your phone numbers (main, WhatsApp, alternate). Google shows one but tracks all of them.
  • Neweb keeps your GBP hours and description in sync with your website automatically. No manual double-entry.
03

Build local citations on Indian directories

A citation is any mention of your business name, address, and phone number on the web, even without a link. Google uses citations to confirm that your business exists and is real. In India, the top local citation sources are different from the USA or UK. You need to build a presence on Indian platforms in particular.

Priority Indian citation sources (in order of authority): JustDial, IndiaMart, Sulekha, IndiaYellowPages, TradeIndia, NearFox, AskLaila, UrbanClap (now Urban Company), and industry-specific directories like Practo for healthcare, Zomato for restaurants, or MagicBricks for real estate. For each, create or claim your listing with identical NAP and link to your website.

  • JustDial is the most important single citation source in India. Claim and verify your listing even if you get no leads from it.
  • IndiaMart is vital for any B2B or manufacturing business. It also drives consumer enquiries in some categories.
  • Sulekha is strong for service businesses (electricians, plumbers, tutors, event planners)
  • Industry-specific directories carry more weight than general ones. A hospital on Practo ranks better for healthcare searches than one only on JustDial.
  • Budget 2 to 3 hours to build 10 quality citations. This is a one-time investment that pays off for years.
04

Build a systematic review strategy

After Google Business Profile completeness, reviews are the most active and most powerful local SEO factor. Their number, quality, and recency all feed Google's local ranking algorithm. So do your replies to them. A business with 50 reviews at 4.7 stars will nearly always outrank a rival with 8 reviews at 4.9 stars, all else equal.

Build a review system. After every sale, send a WhatsApp message with your Google review link. Keep it personal, not automated, for small volumes. For physical shops, place a printed QR code on the counter, packaging, and receipts. For service businesses, follow up one day after the service. Respond to every review within 48 hours. Thank positive reviewers personally. For negative ones, acknowledge the issue and offer to fix it.

  • Create a "review request" WhatsApp message template: "Hi [name], thank you for visiting [shop name]! If you're happy, a Google review means the world to us: [link]. It takes 1 minute."
  • Your reply to a negative review is read by hundreds of future customers. Respond professionally, acknowledge the issue, and invite them to contact you directly.
  • Never buy fake reviews. Google's review fraud detection in India has improved a lot, and businesses caught face listing suspension.
  • Track your review count and rating monthly. Set a goal of 5 new reviews per month as a baseline.
05

Optimise for local keywords on your website

Your website content must tell Google where you are and what you do. So include your city, area, and neighbourhood in your page titles, H1 headings, meta descriptions, and body copy. Do this naturally, not as keyword-stuffed filler. A dentist in Koramangala, Bengaluru should use a page title like "Dentist in Koramangala, Bengaluru | SmileCare Dental Clinic". The homepage opening paragraph should mention Bengaluru and Koramangala within the first 100 words.

Do you serve multiple areas? Create a separate landing page for each one: /dentist-koramangala, /dentist-indiranagar, /dentist-whitefield. Each page should have unique content, not copied from the others. Add localised photos if possible, and an embedded Google Map centred on that area. These area pages rank for hyper-local searches that your homepage cannot target. If you do not have a website yet, see how affordable it is in our guide to website cost in India.   • Use Google Search Console (free) to see which queries drive impressions to your site. Optimize for the ones where you rank 4 to 15.
  • Include your neighbourhood name, not just the city. "Near Juhu Beach" ranks better for Juhu-area searches than just "Mumbai".
  • Add LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema markup to your homepage and contact page. Neweb does this automatically.
  • Add your opening hours to your website footer. Google reads footer text, and it helps link hours to your listing.
  • Include your address in your website footer in text form (not an image) so Google can read it
06

Add Hindi and regional language pages

Hundreds of millions of Indians use the internet mainly in regional languages (IAMAI, 2023). Android is the main device for Indian internet users. On it, voice search happens largely in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi, Bengali, and other regional languages. A business with only English content is invisible to this huge group of searchers.

You do not need to translate your entire website. Start with your homepage and your most important service or product page. Use hreflang tags to tell Google which language each page version targets. For Hindi, the URL structure /hi/ works well (e.g., yoursite.in/hi/). Write Hindi content in the Devanagari script. Romanised Hindi (Hindi words in English letters) does not rank for vernacular voice searches.

  • Hindi "near me" queries like "मेरे पास रेस्टोरेंट" (restaurant near me) have exploded with voice search. A Hindi page targeting this keyword can drive significant traffic.
  • Neweb supports 14 Indian and international languages with one-click publishing and automatic hreflang tagging
  • Google Translate is a starting point for Hindi content, but have a Hindi-fluent speaker review it. Mistranslations damage trust.
  • Businesses in Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and West Bengal benefit especially from regional language content. These states have strong preferences for vernacular content.
  • Add your city name in the regional language to your GBP description too. It helps with vernacular local searches.

FAQ

Common questions.

How long does local SEO take to work in India?

Local SEO works on different timelines depending on the activity. Google Business Profile optimisation and NAP fixes usually show up in Maps rankings within 2 to 4 weeks, because Google reindexes profile changes quickly. Organic website rankings for local keywords take longer, typically 3 to 6 months of consistent work building content and citations. Reviews are the fastest-acting signal of all: gathering 10 genuine Google reviews can move a small business from invisible to the Local Pack within 30 days in many categories. The exact speed depends on competition, so a salon in a crowded Mumbai market will take longer than a shop in a Tier 3 town. Treat local SEO as compounding rather than instant. A tip: front-load the quick wins, fix NAP, complete your GBP, and start collecting reviews in week one, then keep posting and earning citations every month so your ranking keeps climbing rather than plateauing.

Is JustDial still relevant for local SEO in India?

Yes, JustDial is still relevant for local SEO in India. It remains one of the country's largest local business directories and a significant citation source, and a listing whose name, address, and phone exactly match your Google Business Profile sends trust signals that help Google verify your business. Beyond SEO value, JustDial has its own search traffic and call-based enquiry system, which still drives real leads, particularly in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities where many customers search JustDial directly or call its helpline. Claim and verify your free listing, add photos, and keep your NAP identical to every other platform. You do not need the paid PowerAds package to benefit. Pair JustDial with IndiaMart for B2B, Sulekha for services, and any industry directory such as Practo or Zomato. A tip: respond to JustDial's verification call promptly, because unverified listings rank lower and look less trustworthy to customers.

Should I create a Hindi version of my website for SEO?

Yes, you should create a Hindi or regional-language version if your customers are likely to search in that language, which is true for a large and growing share of Indian internet users. Voice search on Android, the dominant device in India, happens overwhelmingly in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi, and Bengali, so a Hindi landing page targeting queries like "मेरे पास रेस्टोरेंट" (restaurant near me) can capture traffic your English pages miss entirely, often with much lower competition. You do not need to translate the whole site; start with your homepage and top service page, write in proper Devanagari script rather than romanised Hindi, and use hreflang tags with a clear URL structure such as /hi/. Have a fluent speaker review the copy, because machine mistranslations hurt trust. States like Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and West Bengal especially reward vernacular content. A tip: add your city name in the regional language to your Google Business description too, so it surfaces in vernacular local searches.

What is the most important local SEO factor for Indian businesses?

The most important local SEO factor for Indian businesses is a fully optimised Google Business Profile, paired with a steady stream of genuine customer reviews. The Local Pack is the three-business box at the top of local search results, and it captures a large share of all clicks (BrightLocal, 2023). A complete GBP will outrank most competitors in Maps, even those with older or more authoritative websites. To get there, set the right primary category, accurate hours, photos, regular Posts, and 20 or more reviews averaging above four stars. Reviews carry extra weight because their number, quality, recency, and your replies all feed the ranking algorithm. After GBP and reviews, the next priority is NAP consistency across your website and Indian directories like JustDial. This combination is the best use of your first five hours of local SEO effort. A tip: never buy fake reviews, since Google's fraud detection in India can suspend your listing entirely.
