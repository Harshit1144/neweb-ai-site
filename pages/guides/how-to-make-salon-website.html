How to make a website for your salon or beauty parlour.

⏱ 5 min read 📅 Updated June 2026 ✍ By Neweb team

India's beauty and personal care market is growing fast (Statista, 2023) and is increasingly discovered online. A salon website that shows your work, lists your prices, and makes booking easy converts "salon near me" searches into appointments, even while you are busy with a client in the chair. Here is how to build one in 6 steps.

01

Register your salon domain

Salon domain names follow a simple formula: [salon name] + salon/beauty/parlour + [city/area].in. Examples: glosssalonbandra.in, shearmagicjaipur.com, naturalbeautyparlourpune.in. The area name is especially valuable for salons because "salon in Koramangala" is a very high-intent search query — people do not travel far for beauty services.

For salons targeting premium customers, a clean short name without the area (e.g., gloss.in or elara.in) can signal higher-end positioning. For neighbourhood salons and beauty parlours where locality matters, include the area name in both the domain and the page title. Both approaches work; choose based on your positioning.

  • Check availability at Neweb's domain checker
  • Neweb includes your domain free every year — domain renewal is one less cost to track
  • If your salon name is two or more words, test both hyphenated and unhyphenated versions (hyphens are fine in domains but reduce word-of-mouth recall slightly)
02

Build a complete service menu with prices and duration

The service menu is the most-visited page on any salon website by a significant margin. Customers arrive to answer one question: "What services do you offer, how long will it take, and what does it cost?" A complete, clearly formatted service menu with all three answers drives more bookings than any promotional content or testimonials can.

Organise services by category: Hair (Haircut, Colour, Highlights, Smoothening, Keratin), Skin (Facials, Clean-Up, Threading, Waxing), Nails, Makeup, Bridal Packages, and Men's Grooming if applicable. For each service, include the price (or starting price for variable-length services), duration in minutes, and the product brand used if it is a differentiator (Schwarzkopf, Kerastase, L'Oreal Professionnel).

  • Show prices even if they are starting prices — "Haircut from ₹299 (varies by hair length)" is better than "call for pricing"
  • Add duration estimates — "60 min" next to a facial helps customers plan their visit and values their time
  • Highlight your most popular services with a "Most popular" badge — it reduces decision fatigue for new customers
  • Add a "Bridal Packages" section even as a separate page — bridal is high-value and has dedicated search intent
03

Add an online booking system or WhatsApp booking flow

The booking system is what converts a website visitor into a paying customer. For most Indian salons, a WhatsApp click-to-chat button with a pre-filled booking message is the most effective and lowest-friction option: customers tap it, send a message with their name and desired service, and your staff confirms via WhatsApp. No app, no account creation, no complicated form.

If you want calendar-based booking with automatic reminders, Fresha (formerly Shedul) is the most popular booking platform for Indian salons — it is free for basic use and integrates with WhatsApp confirmations. Link your Fresha "Book Now" button from your website. Google also has a "Book" button on GBP for salons using supported booking partners. Both drive incremental bookings beyond WhatsApp.

  • Pre-fill your WhatsApp button message: "Hi, I'd like to book a [service] appointment at [Salon Name]. My preferred date is ___ and time is ___"
  • Fresha, Setmore, and Vagaro are popular booking platforms used by Indian salons — all have free tiers
  • Reduce booking friction: every additional step between "I want to book" and "appointment confirmed" loses a meaningful share of potential bookings
  • For bridal and other high-value slots, take a deposit — see how to accept UPI payments on your website
  • Send a WhatsApp reminder 24 hours before the appointment; it sharply reduces no-shows
04

Create a before/after gallery — your most powerful marketing asset

Before/after transformation photos are the single most converting content type for any salon or beauty business. They answer the customer's fundamental question — "Can this salon actually do what I want?" — with undeniable visual evidence. A salon website with a strong before/after gallery converts at a much higher rate than one that only lists services and prices.

Shoot before photos at the start of the service and after photos immediately after completion — consistent lighting (natural light near a window), consistent background (the salon chair or a plain wall), and consistent crop (shoulders-up for hair, close-up for nails or skin). Organise your gallery by service type: Hair Transformations, Hair Colour, Bridal Makeup, Skincare, Nail Art. Always get the client's written or WhatsApp consent before posting.

  • Photograph your work after every remarkable transformation — over 6 months you will have a gallery of 50+ real results
  • Post your before/after photos on Instagram first, then embed them on your website gallery — this grows both channels simultaneously
  • Add a caption with the service name and products used: "Balayage + Kerastase treatment — 3 hours, ₹4,500"
  • Ask satisfied clients if they are happy for their transformation to be shared — most are proud of the result and say yes
  • Always get consent — posting without consent, even if the client loved the result, can damage trust if they later object
05

Integrate your Instagram feed on your website

Salons are one of the most visual business categories, and Instagram is where Indian beauty consumers spend significant time discovering new looks. Embedding your latest Instagram posts on your website kills two birds with one stone: it keeps your website looking fresh without manual updates, and it shows visitors that your salon is active and posting real work regularly.

Most website builders including Neweb support Instagram feed embeds that auto-update when you post on Instagram. Place the embed on your homepage or gallery page. The feed should show your last 9–12 posts in a clean grid. Each post clicking through to Instagram also grows your followers — website visitors who like your work will follow you for ongoing inspiration, creating a long-term marketing relationship.

  • Post on Instagram at least 3x per week — your website feed only looks good if your Instagram is regularly updated
  • Use Instagram Reels for time-lapse transformations — they get far more reach than static photos currently
  • Add your Instagram handle prominently on your website: "Follow us @yoursalonname for daily inspiration"
  • Include service-specific hashtags on your Instagram posts: #bangaloresalon #mumbaihairsalon #bridalmakeup — these drive local discovery
06

Set up Google Business Profile for your salon

Customers searching "salon near me," "best haircut in [area]," or "bridal makeup [city]" see Google Maps results at the top of the page. A verified, complete GBP listing for your salon is what puts you in those results. In the beauty services category, GBP is the dominant discovery channel for local customers who have not previously visited your salon.

Create your GBP listing with the primary category "Beauty Salon" or more specifically "Hair Salon" or "Nail Salon" as appropriate. Add every service in the Services section — Google uses these to match your listing to service-specific searches. Upload at least 20 photos: interior, styling stations, product displays, team, and before/after results. Enable the Booking link if you use a supported platform like Fresha. Our full walkthrough on how to get a Google Business listing covers verification step by step, and our guide to ranking on Google Maps in India explains how to move up the local pack.

  • Set your category specifically — "Hair Salon" outranks "Beauty Salon" for hair-specific searches, and you can add both as primary + secondary
  • Add every service in your GBP Services section: Haircut, Colour, Highlights, Facial, Waxing, Bridal Makeup, etc.
  • Post before/after photos as GBP photos — these are the most clicked photos on beauty business listings
  • Use Google Business Posts to promote seasonal offers: "Festive season special — 20% off bridal packages booked before Diwali"
  • Collect Google reviews systematically — beauty salons benefit enormously from high review counts; aim for 50+ within your first year

FAQ

Common questions.

Do salons in India need a website or is Instagram enough?

You need both, because they do different jobs. Instagram is excellent for discovery and showing your work through Reels and before/after posts, but it does not rank on Google Search or Google Maps when someone types "salon near me" or "best haircut in Koramangala". A website gives you that Maps presence, a complete service menu with prices, a WhatsApp booking hub, and a web address you own rather than rent from a platform. The smart approach is to link the two: embed your Instagram feed on your website so it always looks fresh, and put your website link in your Instagram bio so followers can see prices and book. In India, where customers shortlist on Instagram but confirm on WhatsApp and Google, running both together converts far better than either alone. Many salons add a UPI deposit link on the site to cut no-shows.

Should I show prices on my salon website?

Yes, showing prices is the single biggest driver of online salon bookings in India. Customers who see prices upfront are more likely to book and far less likely to cancel, because there is no surprise at the counter. If prices vary by hair length, skin type, or complexity, show a clear starting price such as "Haircut from ₹299" or "Bridal makeup from ₹6,000" with a short note that the final amount is confirmed at the salon after consultation. Group services into Hair, Skin, Nails, and Bridal so customers compare easily. Mention the product brands you use, like L'Oreal Professionnel or Kerastase, because they justify premium pricing. For high-value bridal packages, list what is included rather than just a number. A tip: add a "Most popular" badge on two or three services to guide first-time customers and reduce decision fatigue.

What photos should a salon website have?

Before and after transformation photos are the highest-converting content for any salon, because they prove you can deliver the look a customer wants. Beyond those, include your salon interior and styling stations, product shelves with recognisable brands, friendly team photos, and close-up shots of hair colour, nail art, threading, and skincare work. Shoot in natural light near a window with a consistent background and crop so the gallery looks professional and trustworthy. Organise photos by service type: Hair Transformations, Hair Colour, Bridal Makeup, Skincare, and Nails. Always get the client's written or WhatsApp consent before posting, even if they loved the result, because reposting without permission can break trust. Post your best work on Instagram first, then embed it on the website so both channels grow together. Aim for at least twenty photos on your Google Business Profile too, since those get the most clicks.

How do I get bookings from my salon website?

The most effective booking tool for Indian salons is a WhatsApp click-to-chat button with a pre-filled message, because most customers prefer WhatsApp over downloading a booking app. Set the message to something like "Hi, I'd like to book a haircut at [Salon Name] on ___ at ___" so the customer only has to fill the blanks. Add a short enquiry form on the site that also routes to WhatsApp for those who prefer typing. If you want calendar-based slots with automatic reminders, link a free Fresha profile, which integrates WhatsApp confirmations and can power the Book button on your Google Business Profile. Reduce friction at every step, since each extra click between intent and confirmation loses a meaningful share of bookings. Send a WhatsApp reminder 24 hours before the appointment to cut no-shows, and consider a small UPI deposit for high-value bridal slots.
