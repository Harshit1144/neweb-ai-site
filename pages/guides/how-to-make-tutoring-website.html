How to make a website for your coaching centre or tuition classes.

⏱ 5 min read 📅 Updated June 2026 ✍ By Neweb team

India has over a million coaching centres, and the ones that consistently fill their batches are increasingly the ones that are findable on Google before admission season. A well-built coaching website with results, batch info, and WhatsApp enquiry converts parents into enrolments without a single cold call. Here is how to build it in 6 steps.

01

Register your coaching centre domain

Your domain should reflect your institute name and ideally hint at what you teach. Examples: brightfuturecoaching.in, iitjeeprep.in, aimersacademynagpur.com. If your coaching centre name is already well-known locally, keep the domain exactly as the name. For new institutes, adding the subject or city to the domain (e.g., sciencetutorspune.in) helps with search visibility.

Coaching centre websites are searched by parents and students using very specific queries: "best NEET coaching in [city]," "CBSE Class 10 tuition near [area]," "IIT JEE foundation [city]." Your domain and page URLs should reflect these search patterns where possible. A URL like yoursite.in/neet-coaching-hyderabad ranks better than yoursite.in/courses for that specific query.

  • Register a .in domain for maximum local India SEO benefit — it is also cheaper than .com
  • Check availability with Neweb's free domain checker before committing to a name
  • If you teach multiple boards or entrance exams, keep the domain general (institute name) and create specific sub-pages per exam
02

Create a detailed batch and courses listing page

The batch listing page is the most visited and most functionally important page on any coaching website. Parents arrive with a specific question: "Does this centre teach Class 12 PCM? What are the timings? How much does it cost? Are there still seats?" A page that answers all of these questions completely eliminates the need for the initial phone call and accelerates the enrolment decision significantly.

Structure each batch entry with: class level, board (CBSE/ICSE/State Board), subjects covered, teacher name and qualification, batch timings (morning/evening options), monthly fee, annual fee with any advance payment discount, batch size, seats remaining, and a "Register Interest" or "Enquire on WhatsApp" button. Update seat availability regularly — "Only 3 seats left" is a genuine urgency signal that converts well.

  • Create separate sub-pages for each major category: School Tuition, JEE Coaching, NEET Coaching, Foundation (Class 6–8), Olympiad Preparation
  • Include a fee structure table — parents compare fees across 3–5 coaching centres before deciding
  • Add a "Demo Class" option if you offer it — free trial classes are a major differentiator for new enrolments
  • Let parents pay registration or the first instalment online — see how to accept UPI payments on your website
  • Show teacher qualification on the batch page, not just the faculty page — it influences the decision at the point of reading
03

Build a prominent student results page

The results page is the single most powerful converting page on any coaching centre website. It serves as social proof in the most concrete form possible: "These real students, from these specific classes, scored these specific marks after attending our coaching." No testimonial, no brochure, no paid advertisement comes close to the trust a strong results page builds with prospective parents.

Organise results by year and exam: Board Results 2026, JEE Mains 2026 Selections, NEET 2026 Qualifiers. For each result, include the student's name and photo (with parental consent), the exam, the score or rank, and optionally the school they attended. Use actual marks — "98.2% in CBSE Class 12" is more credible than "Distinction with high scores." Design the page with a grid or cards layout that feels celebratory rather than statistical.

  • Always get written parental consent before displaying a minor's name, photo, or academic results on your website or social media
  • Under the DPDP Act 2023, minors' data requires explicit parental consent — include this in your enrolment form
  • Update the results page every year during result season (May–July for CBSE/ICSE, June for JEE/NEET) — fresh results are a major trust signal
  • Include a "Our track record" counter on your homepage: "Over 1,200 students helped since 2018 — 94% scored above 85%"
  • Link your results page prominently from the homepage — it is the proof point that converts fence-sitters
04

Set up a WhatsApp enquiry flow

In India, the vast majority of coaching centre enquiries happen on WhatsApp, not email, not phone calls. Parents prefer to ask questions asynchronously, review batch details in their own time, and ask follow-up questions over several days before making a decision. A well-designed WhatsApp enquiry flow captures these leads before they move to the next coaching centre on the Google results page.

Add a floating WhatsApp button to every page with a pre-filled message like: "Hello, I'm interested in admission to [Coaching Name]. My child is in Class __. Can you share batch details?" This gives your staff context before they even open the message. Link this button from your batch listing page, your results page, and your homepage CTA section.

  • Set up WhatsApp Business Quick Replies for: "Batch timings and fees," "Demo class request," "JEE batch details," "NEET batch details," "How to enrol"
  • Create a WhatsApp broadcast list for interested parents who have not yet enrolled — send batch start date reminders and demo class invitations
  • Add a simple enquiry form as a backup for parents who prefer not to use WhatsApp — send the form submission to your email and WhatsApp simultaneously
  • Respond to WhatsApp enquiries within 2 hours during business hours — slow response is the top reason enquiries convert to a competitor
05

Collect and display parent and student testimonials

Parent testimonials work differently from student results — they address the emotional dimension of the enrolment decision. A result proves competence; a testimonial from a parent proves that the coaching centre is caring, communicative, and safe for their child. Both matter, but they answer different questions in the parent's mind.

Ask parents for testimonials at two natural moments: when their child achieves a significant result (board exam, entrance test), and after completing the academic year. The most effective format is a short paragraph (100–150 words) with the parent's first name, their child's class and result, and ideally a photo. Video testimonials are even more powerful — a 45-second parent speaking to camera on their phone converts better than any text testimonial.

  • Ask for specific testimonials: "Our teacher explained the concept 3 times patiently until my son understood" is more convincing than "Great coaching centre"
  • Video testimonials do not need professional production — a well-lit selfie video from a parent's phone is authentic and effective
  • Add a "Star rating" to your testimonials section alongside the parent quote
  • Student testimonials for Class 10+ students (with their own consent) are also powerful — hearing from peers is influential for students evaluating coaching options
06

Set up Google Business for your coaching centre

Parents searching "NEET coaching near me" or "best tuition centre in [area]" see Google Business Profile results at the top of the page. The education category on Google Business is highly searched in India, particularly in the March–July admission season. A well-maintained GBP listing significantly increases walk-in and phone enquiries from first-time parents who have never heard of your coaching centre.

Create or claim your GBP listing with the category "Tutoring Service" or "Education Center" as appropriate. List every subject and exam you cover in the Services section. Add photos of your classrooms, whiteboards, study materials, and any awards or certificates. Post your results every year as a Google Post — "JEE 2026: 14 of our students qualified from our Koramangala centre" drives significant local attention during result season. Step-by-step help is in our guide on how to get a Google Business listing, and our local SEO guide for India shows how to win hyper-local "coaching near me" searches.

  • List your subjects and boards explicitly in the GBP description: "CBSE, ICSE, and State Board tuition for Class 6–12. JEE and NEET foundation and advanced batches."
  • Add all your teaching locations if you have multiple centres — each location should have its own GBP listing
  • Run a Google Business Post every admission season (April–June) with batch start dates and a "Limited seats remaining" urgency message
  • Respond promptly to parent questions in the GBP Q&A section — it signals responsiveness to parents evaluating you

FAQ

Common questions.

What should a coaching centre website include?

A coaching centre website should include a Home page with your USP and a clear contact button, a Courses or Batches page listing class level, board, subjects, timings, fees, and seats remaining, a Results page showing board marks and JEE or NEET ranks with student photos, Faculty profiles with qualifications, Parent testimonials, and a Contact or Enquiry form. A floating WhatsApp button is essential, because in India the vast majority of admission enquiries happen on WhatsApp rather than email or phone. Add a Demo Class request option, since free trial classes are a strong differentiator, and show seat availability to create genuine urgency. Build separate sub-pages for each major offering, such as JEE Coaching, NEET Coaching, and School Tuition, so each ranks for queries like "NEET coaching in Kota". A tip: link your Results page from the homepage, because it is the proof point that converts parents who are comparing several centres.

How do I show student results on my website legally?

To show student results legally, get written consent from each student and their parents before displaying any name, photo, or marks. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, publishing the personal data of minors requires explicit, verifiable parental consent, so a verbal yes is not enough. The simplest way to handle this is a consent checkbox in your enrolment form that reads "I agree to my child's results being featured on the institute's website and marketing materials", kept on file for each student. Use real, specific figures such as "98.2% in CBSE Class 12" rather than vague phrases, because specificity builds trust, but only for students who have consented. Refresh the page every result season, around May to July for boards and June for JEE and NEET. A tip: if a parent later withdraws consent, remove that student promptly from the website and any social posts to stay compliant and maintain goodwill.

Should I list fees on my coaching centre website?

Yes, you should list fees on your coaching centre website, because parents actively compare three to five centres before they ever call. Showing fees upfront builds trust and filters out enquiries from families outside your range, so your staff spend time on serious leads. Present both the monthly and annual fee, mention any advance-payment discount, and if amounts vary by batch or board, show a clear range such as "₹2,500 to ₹4,000 per month". If you offer merit scholarships, note that they are available and state the selection criteria, since this attracts strong students. A simple fee table per batch works best for quick comparison. You can also let parents pay registration or the first instalment online by UPI to reduce drop-off. A tip: pair the fee with the teacher's qualification and batch size on the same page, because parents judge value, not just price.

How do I compete with BYJU's and Vedantu online?

Local coaching centres compete with online giants like BYJU's and Vedantu on trust, personal attention, and proximity, not on the size of a content library. Your website should lead with local credibility: teacher qualifications, the number of years you have operated in the area, board-specific results for CBSE, ICSE, and your State Board, and authentic parent and student testimonials. Emphasise small batch sizes, doubt-clearing sessions, parent-teacher communication, and a physical centre parents can visit, all of which large online platforms cannot match. Optimise for hyper-local searches such as "Class 10 tuition near [area]" with dedicated pages and a verified Google Business Profile so you appear in the local pack. Offer a free demo class to convert fence-sitters. A tip: post your results each year as a Google Post during admission season, because nothing reassures a local parent more than seeing neighbours' children succeed at your centre.
