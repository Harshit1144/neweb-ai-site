Why a clean URL slug matters for SEO

A URL slug is the readable tail of a web address — the best-cafe-in-bandra in yoursite.in/best-cafe-in-bandra. It is small, but it does a surprising amount of work. The slug is one of the first things both Google and a human see, and getting it right is one of the cheapest on-page SEO wins available to any Indian small business.

Search engines read the words in your URL as a topic signal. A slug like /diamond-rings-jaipur tells Google, at a glance, exactly what the page is about — before it has even parsed the content. Compare that to a default CMS slug like /product?id=48217 or /page-2, which says nothing. A keyword-rich, descriptive slug reinforces relevance for the search terms you actually want to rank for.

Clean slugs also lift click-through rates. When your URL appears in the Google results, on a WhatsApp forward, or printed on a flyer, yoursite.in/wedding-catering-pune looks trustworthy and tells the customer where they are going. A messy string of numbers and capital letters looks broken or spammy, and people hesitate to click. Higher click-through is itself a positive ranking signal, so a good slug compounds.

How this slug generator works

  1. Type your title. Paste a blog headline, product name, or service title — for example "Best Biryani in Hyderabad" or "Dr. Sharma's Dental Clinic, Ind-iranagar".

  2. Accents and punctuation are stripped. The tool transliterates accented and diacritic characters (é becomes e, ï becomes i), removes apostrophes, commas, and other symbols, and collapses multiple spaces into one separator.

  3. Everything goes lowercase. Because URLs can be case-sensitive on some servers, the slug is forced to lowercase to avoid duplicate-content and split-link problems.

  4. Stop words are dropped (optional). With the checkbox on, filler words like "the", "and", "of", "in" and "for" are removed so the slug stays short and keyword-dense. Turn it off to keep the title verbatim.

  5. Length is trimmed. Set a max length and the tool cuts cleanly at a word boundary so you never end up with a half-word at the end of the slug.

URL slug best practices

  • Keep it short. Three to five meaningful words is the sweet spot. Long slugs get truncated in search results and are harder to share. Under 60 characters is a good ceiling.
  • Lead with the keyword. Put the term you want to rank for early. /seo-services-mumbai is stronger than /our-affordable-and-trusted-seo-services-in-mumbai.
  • Use hyphens, not underscores. Google reads hyphens as spaces between words. Underscores join words into one token, so jewellery_shop can be read as a single, meaningless string.
  • Always lowercase. Mixed-case URLs risk resolving to two different pages on case-sensitive servers, splitting your ranking signals.
  • Drop stop words. "a", "the", "of", "and" rarely add SEO value in a slug. Removing them keeps the URL tight and keyword-rich.
  • Avoid dates and IDs where you can. /diwali-offers can be reused every year; /diwali-offers-2024 looks stale by 2026 and may need redirects.
  • Do not change live slugs casually. Once a page ranks, changing its slug breaks the URL. If you must, set up a 301 redirect from the old slug to the new one.

Frequently asked questions

What is a URL slug and why does it matter for SEO?

A URL slug is the part of a web address that identifies a specific page in a human-readable way, for example the "best-biryani-in-hyderabad" in yoursite.in/best-biryani-in-hyderabad. It matters because a clean, keyword-rich slug helps Google understand the page topic, improves click-through from search results, and is easier for customers to read, remember, and share on WhatsApp.

Should I use hyphens or underscores in URL slugs?

Use hyphens. Google treats hyphens as word separators, so "jewellery-shop-jaipur" is read as three distinct words. Underscores are treated as joiners, so "jewellery_shop_jaipur" can be read as a single token. For SEO, hyphens are the long-standing best practice. This generator defaults to hyphens but lets you switch to underscores if your platform requires it.

How long should a URL slug be?

Shorter is generally better. Aim for three to five meaningful words and keep the slug under about 60 characters. Long slugs get truncated in search results and look spammy. Removing stop words like "the", "and", "of" and "in" keeps the slug tight while preserving the keywords that actually matter for ranking.

Should URL slugs be lowercase?

Yes, always use lowercase. On many servers, URLs are case-sensitive, so /Best-Cafe and /best-cafe can resolve to different pages and split your link equity or cause duplicate-content issues. Lowercase slugs avoid this entirely and are the universal convention. This tool converts everything to lowercase automatically.
